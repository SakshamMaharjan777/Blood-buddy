Blood for every Nepali, when it matters

BloodBuddy is a web-based blood donor management system built for Nepal — connecting donors, requesters, and hospitals on one trusted platform.

Our mission

To make finding safe, compatible blood a matter of minutes, not hours — replacing scattered social media appeals with a verified, searchable donor registry backed by real-time hospital inventories.

Our vision

A Nepal where no life is lost waiting for blood — where every district is covered, every hospital's stock is visible, and every eligible donor can be reached the moment they're needed.

The problem today
  • Blood requests are posted on Facebook and WhatsApp, with no guarantee of reaching compatible donors.
  • There is no searchable, verified donor database by blood group and district.
  • Hospitals keep manual blood bank records — no real-time visibility.
  • Duplicate, fraudulent, and outdated requests go unmoderated.
How BloodBuddy helps
  • Searchable registry of available donors by blood group and location.
  • Automated email alerts to nearby donors when urgent requests are submitted.
  • Hospital portals with live inventory and request queues.
  • Central admin moderation to keep requests genuine and profiles current.

What we stand for

Four principles guide how BloodBuddy is built and run.

Voluntary
Built around voluntary, non-remunerated donation — the WHO-recommended model.
Verified
Donor profiles, hospital accounts, and requests are reviewed by administrators.
Fast
Minutes from request to match — because in emergencies, every minute counts.
Local
Designed for Nepal — districts, municipalities, and our partner hospitals.
