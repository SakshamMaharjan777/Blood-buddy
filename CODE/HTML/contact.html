Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you write to us.

Use the Register button and pick "Blood Donor". You'll enter your blood group, district, and availability — the whole form takes about two minutes.
As soon as a requester submits a matching urgent request, an email alert goes out to available donors with that blood group in the district. How quickly you respond is up to you — most matches happen within the hour.
Only your first name, blood group, and general area are shown to logged-in requesters searching for donors. Your phone number and email are never shared publicly.
Choose "Hospital partnership" in the form above, or email hello@bloodbuddy.np. Partner hospitals get a dedicated portal for inventory management and a private API for their blood bank systems.
For whole blood, Nepal follows the standard 90-day interval between donations. BloodBuddy tracks your last donation and shows your next eligible date on your dashboard.
