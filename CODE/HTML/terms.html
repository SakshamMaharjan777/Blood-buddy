Terms of Service

The ground rules for using BloodBuddy — what the platform does, what it doesn't, and what we expect from every member.

Demo document for an academic project
Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. About BloodBuddy

BloodBuddy is a web-based blood donor management system for Nepal. It helps compatible blood donors, requesters, and partner hospital blood banks find each other quickly — replacing unstructured social-media appeals with a searchable, role-based platform.

BloodBuddy is a demonstration built for an academic project (CPP400, Asia e University) and is not a commercially deployed medical service. Nothing on this platform constitutes medical advice.

2. Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years old to register as a blood donor, in line with blood-transfusion guidelines in Nepal.
  • Requesters and hospital staff accounts may be created on behalf of an organisation or a patient's family.
  • You agree to provide accurate details — blood group, contact information, and location — because wrong data can delay a real match.

3. Donor responsibilities

  • Keep your availability status up to date and only accept requests you genuinely intend to fulfil.
  • Donating is always voluntary; you may decline any request without giving a reason.
  • Final eligibility to donate is decided at the hospital blood bank after screening — acceptance on BloodBuddy is not medical clearance.

4. Requester responsibilities

  • Submit requests only for genuine patient needs, with accurate units, urgency, and hospital details.
  • Cancel requests through the platform when blood is found elsewhere so donors are not left waiting.
  • Do not use the platform for commercial plasma/blood trading, which is illegal in Nepal.

5. Hospital accounts

Hospital staff accounts manage their institution's blood bank inventory and respond to forwarded requests. Inventory levels shown are the responsibility of the hospital account holder, and partner hospitals are expected to keep stock data current.

6. Acceptable use

  • Do not harass, spam, or pressure donors through platform contact details.
  • Do not impersonate another person, hospital, or BloodBuddy staff.
  • Do not attempt to scrape, overload, or reverse-engineer the platform.

Accounts that break these rules may be suspended by administrators — in the live system this is done from the Admin Portal's user-management table.

7. Platform availability

We aim to keep BloodBuddy reachable at all times, but the service is provided "as is" without warranties. For urgent, life-threatening situations always contact your nearest hospital or the National Blood Transfusion Service directly — do not rely on any single platform.

8. Limitation of liability

BloodBuddy connects people; it does not collect, test, store, or transport blood. The platform is not liable for outcomes of donations, transfusions, or communications arranged between members. Hospitals remain solely responsible for screening, compatibility testing, and the donation procedure itself.

9. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated as the platform evolves. Significant changes will be announced on the site, and the "Last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms? Reach us through the contact page or email hello@bloodbuddy.np.
