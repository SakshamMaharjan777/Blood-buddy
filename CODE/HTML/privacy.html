Privacy Policy

2. How your data is used

  • To match blood requests with compatible, nearby donors and notify you by email (SMTP) when your group is urgently needed.
  • To let hospitals fulfil and verify donations and keep inventory levels accurate.
  • To let administrators keep the platform safe — suspending accounts that violate the Terms of Service.

We never sell your data, and there are no advertisers on BloodBuddy.

3. What is public on your profile

Requesters can see your first name, blood group, district, and availability while you're marked available. Your email, phone number, and full donation history are never shown publicly — they are only shared when you accept a specific request, so the hospital can reach you.

4. Images

Profile and event photos you upload are stored securely in the database as binary data and served only to logged-in members. You can remove an uploaded photo at any time from your profile page.

5. Cookies & local storage

This demo stores your session and demo data (requests, inventory, users) in your browser's local storage under the bb_ prefix — nothing is sent to a server. In the live system, session cookies would be http-only and expire with your login.

6. Data retention & deletion

You can request account deletion at any time via the contact page. Donations already completed are kept as anonymised statistical records (blood group and month only) so hospitals can plan blood drives.

7. Children's data

BloodBuddy is not directed at children under 18. Donor registration requires users to confirm they are 18 or older, in line with Nepal's blood-transfusion guidelines.

8. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text. Access to medical-adjacent data is restricted by role — donors see their own history, hospitals see inventory and requests routed to them, and administrators see only what moderation requires.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way we handle data changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions? Use the contact page or email privacy@bloodbuddy.np.
