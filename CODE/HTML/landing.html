How it works
From request to donor in minutes
Three simple steps connect people in need with verified, available donors across Nepal.
Step 1

Register your profile

Sign up as a donor with your blood group, district, and availability. Takes under 2 minutes.

Step 2

Search & match

Requesters filter by blood group and location to find compatible, available donors instantly.

Step 3

Get notified

Donors receive email alerts when an urgent request matches their blood group and district.

Blood group compatibility
Know your compatible groups
A quick reference — used by our matching algorithm to suggest compatible donors when an exact match isn't available.
AB+
Universal recipient — accepts all blood groups
O-
Universal donor — compatible with all recipients
A+ / A-
Can receive from A and O type donors
B+ / B-
Can receive from B and O type donors
